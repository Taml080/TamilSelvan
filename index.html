About Me

Crafting Digital Stories That Convert

I'm Tamil Selvan I — a passionate Digital Marketing Executive based in Erode, Tamil Nadu. Currently completing my BCA at Shree Venkateshwara Arts and Science College, I've been building real-world marketing expertise across SEO, paid media, and content strategy.

My journey spans internships at Virtouspark (Bangalore) and KA Strategist (Coimbatore), plus a hands-on project with Kalvippaathai, where I managed Meta Ads campaigns, created content strategies, and generated qualified leads.

Currently, I’m working at Minerva Industries, where I handle end-to-end digital marketing activities such as SEO, SMM, content creation, social media page handling, AI video generation, Meta Ads, and WhatsApp automation.

I'm deeply passionate about the intersection of AI creativity, data-driven performance marketing, and brand storytelling — always looking for ways to make campaigns smarter and results stronger.
